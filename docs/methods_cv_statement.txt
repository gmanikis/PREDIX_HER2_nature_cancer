--- METHODS PARAGRAPH ---

Prediction performance was evaluated by repeated stratified nested cross-validation. The outer loop was stratified 5-fold cross-validation repeated 200 times with different random partitions (1000 outer test-fold evaluations; each outer training set comprised ~80% of patients and each held-out test fold ~20%). Within every outer training fold, stratified 5-fold inner cross-validation was used for classifier selection, hyperparameter tuning and feature-signature discovery; all preprocessing (imputation, near-zero-variance filtering, correlation pruning, scaling, the univariate outcome screen) was fitted on outer training patients only. Arm-specific models used 5-fold outer cross-validation repeated 100 times (500 evaluations per arm) with 3-fold inner cross-validation. No shuffle-split resampling was used at any stage.

--- FIGURE LEGEND (Extended Data Fig. 11a) ---

Schematic of the repeated stratified nested cross-validation framework. The outer loop is stratified 5-fold cross-validation repeated 200 times (1000 outer evaluations; ~80% training / ~20% testing per fold). Within each outer training fold, stratified 5-fold inner cross-validation performs model selection and feature ranking. The selected model is evaluated on the corresponding held-out outer test fold, and performance is pooled across all 1000 outer evaluations.
